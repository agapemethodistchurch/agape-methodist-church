A LITTLE ABOUT US

More than a building.
A family in Christ.

Agape Methodist Church is a community called to worship God, share the love of Jesus Christ, grow in faith, and serve our neighbours with compassion.

We believe church is where people are encouraged, gifts are nurtured, burdens are shared, and hope is renewed. We are growing together—one prayer, one act of kindness, and one life at a time.
